About

Starting September, I will be a postdoctoral member of the School of Mathematics at the Institute for Advanced Study. I completed my PhD at MIT CSAIL, where I was very fortunate to be advised by Ronitt Rubinfeld.

Research

Broadly, I am interested in algorithms related to machine learning and property testing, and my research is at the intersection of learning theory and sublinear algorithms. More specifically, I am interested in:

  • testing and local reconstruction of Boolean function properties
  • extending learning algorithms to meet demands besides accuracy, and
  • intermediate models between distribution-free and distribution-specific learning.
